Dear Editors of Neuroscience of Consciousness,

I am submitting the manuscript "Balanced-coherence regimes of conscious access: a computational model with public EEG validation" for consideration as a Research Article.

The paper presents the Gamma-Code Consensus (GCC), a computational regime model of access-compatible neural dynamics. The central contributions are: (i) a formally specified bounded-regime model of global access with evaluated predictions in synthetic networks and one model-internal instantiation of selective-preservation re-entry; (ii) a reproducible cross-paradigm empirical pipeline applied to public EEG/PSG datasets, including two independent propofol cohorts, sleep-state geometry, a ketamine boundary-state analysis, a degradation-proxy analysis, and a pilot clinical anchor in disorders of consciousness; and (iii) a frozen validation specification with spectral, artifact, phase-only, lagged-coupling, source-proxy, and reliability controls.

The work is intentionally framed as an organizational and computational model of access-compatible neural dynamics. It does not propose an alternative physical substrate of consciousness and does not make a diagnostic clinical claim.
