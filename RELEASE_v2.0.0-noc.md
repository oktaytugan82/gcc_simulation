# Release v2.0.0-noc

Release date: 2026-05-16

This is the Neuroscience of Consciousness submission snapshot for the GCC manuscript:

> Balanced-coherence regimes of conscious access: a computational model with public EEG validation

## What changed relative to v1.3.3

- Adds the final NoC manuscript source/PDF and two supplements under `manuscript/noc_submission/`.
- Adds the complete author-generated NoC validation package under `noc_release/`.
- Reframes the repository around the final NoC evidence hierarchy rather than the earlier PLOS/Plan-B release history.
- Documents all public datasets re-analysed in the manuscript without redistributing raw third-party data.
- Updates `README.md`, `CITATION.cff`, and `.zenodo.json` to the NoC submission snapshot.

## Included validation batches

- `batch3_cross_dataset_validation`: synthetic validation, Chennu cross-validation, DS005620 replication, Sleep-EDF first-pass validation.
- `batch5_spectral_controls`: bandpower residualization and DS005620 gamma-artifact control.
- `batch6_artifact_lag_controls`: phase surrogate, wPLI, manifest, and run-stability checks.
- `batch7_frozen_transfer_wpli`: frozen cross-dataset benchmark and Chennu wPLI control.
- `batch8_sleep_state_geometry`: Sleep-EDF state-geometry analysis.
- `batch9_bandpower_independence`: bandpower-independence stress tests.
- `batch10_phase_only`: amplitude-normalized phase-only GCC.
- `batch11_csd_source_proxy`: CSD/source-proxy robustness analysis.
- `batch12_degradation_proxy`: DS004504 degradation-proxy analysis.
- `batch13_ketamine`: subanaesthetic ketamine boundary-state analysis.
- `batch14_reliability`: split-half and repeated-run reliability.
- `batch15_gcco_composite`: composite GCCO analyses and sleep residual CV.
- `batch16_doc_pilot`: Mendeley disorders-of-consciousness pilot anchor, residualization, sensitivity, and multiple-comparisons addendum.
- `batch17_chennu_source_proxy`: Chennu template-source/source-proxy GCCO robustness.
- `batch18_sleep_cross_paradigm`: expanded Sleep-EDF cross-paradigm validation.
- `frozen_validation_spec`: frozen feature specification and audit.
- `phase4_evidence_summary`: NoC evidence-summary figure generation.

## Data policy

Raw public datasets are excluded from this repository. Reproduction requires downloading the relevant public datasets from their source archives, as described in the manuscript Data Availability statement. The repository includes scripts, manifests where available, derived result tables, figures, and reports generated by the author.
